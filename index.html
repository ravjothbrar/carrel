    2. Switch on airplane mode, or turn off Wi-Fi, and reload the page.
    3. Add a document and ask a question. You still get a cited answer.

    HOW IT WORKS

    Four steps, all on this device.

    1. Add. Files are split into passages of about 300 tokens and turned into vectors by EmbeddingGemma 2. Images go into the same search.
    2. Ask. Your question is embedded and compared with every passage. If nothing is close enough, carrel says That isn't in your documents and does not call the answer model.
    3. Answer. Gemma 4 writes a short answer from five passages, citing them by number. The passages sit in the margin.
    4. Check. Each sentence of the answer is compared with the passages it cites. Weak matches are marked not clearly supported by your documents.

    WHAT IT IS NOT

    • It is a small model. It can misread a passage, and the check is a similarity score, not proof.
    • Scanned PDFs are not supported: there is no OCR. Audio is not supported.
    • Needs Chrome or Edge with WebGPU. There is no CPU fallback.

    KNOWLEDGE PACK

    A pack is one file with your passages, images and their vectors. Share it, or import it on another device that uses the same embedding model.

    THIS DEVICE

    Clearing removes your documents and settings. Downloaded models stay in the browser cache; clear site data in your browser to remove those too.

    THIS NEEDS A WEBGPU BROWSER

    carrel runs Gemma on your graphics chip, and this browser can't offer one.

    Try current Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop. There is no slower fallback: a model this size on a CPU would take minutes per answer.
